Who sells both seats and AI?

Add-on vs mandatory — how competitors package AI alongside platform / seat fees, and where monday is different.

May 2026 · AI Pricing board · trial research · pricing page captures

Key distinction

Two ways to sell AI alongside seats

Optional add-on

Buy platform first. AI is extra.

  • Customer can purchase paid seats without buying AI
  • AI appears as a separate SKU, section, or line item
  • Platform works fully (or mostly) without AI payment

Examples: ClickUp Brain, M365 Copilot, Notion AI on Plus

Mandatory in core

AI is part of the paid package.

  • Customer cannot buy the paid tier without AI/credits included
  • One checkout decision — AI is bundled, not skippable
  • May show as one price or two line items, but both are required

Examples: Airtable paid tiers, monday new model, Clay Launch+

Hybrid   Some competitors use both models depending on tier (e.g. Notion: add-on on Plus, mandatory in Business+).

Deep dive · mandatory models

Airtable vs monday — both required, different UX

Airtable

Mandatory

Credits bundled inside the seat price — one number at checkout.

  • Team $20/seat includes 15K credits
  • Business $45/seat includes 20K credits
  • Buyer sees one line item; hits credit wall in product
  • Top-ups ~$40/20K (optional, post-purchase)
airtable.com/pricing

monday (new model)

Mandatory

Credits shown as a separate required line item — two components, both mandatory.

  • Platform: seats × $12–19/seat (discounted Y1)
  • AI: mandatory credit bundle ($40–80/mo by tier)
  • Checkout: AI Approach tier → seats → total
  • Cannot complete purchase without both
Both are mandatory. Difference is transparency: Airtable hides credits in one price; monday exposes seat + AI as two explicit checkout decisions.

Implication for monday

monday is in the mandatory camp — but more explicit than peers

Industry default

  • 5 competitors sell AI as optional add-on
  • 4 competitors bundle AI mandatorily in core
  • 3 competitors are hybrid (tier-dependent)
  • Most peers let buyers skip AI entirely

monday's bet

  • Mandatory seat + AI credit bundle
  • Unlike Airtable: two visible line items at checkout
  • Unlike ClickUp: AI is not optional
  • Teaches credit literacy at conversion, not post-pay
Closest mandatory peers: Airtable (hidden bundle), Clay (fixed credit tiers). Closest page-structure peer: ClickUp (two blocks) — but monday makes both blocks required.
